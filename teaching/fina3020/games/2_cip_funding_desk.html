Step 1 of 3: Check Parity & Direction

Round 1 matches the slides’ 90-day HKD/USD example: S = 7.8000, HKD 3%, USD 5%, and zero spread. Quotes stay fixed until you press New Round.

Show hint: compare covered returns

Use T = days / 360 and annual rates as decimals. With HKD per USD quotes, the fair forward is F* = S × (1 + r_HKD × T) / (1 + r_USD × T).

If F is below F*, consider borrowing USD, selling USD spot, investing HKD, and buying USD forward to repay. If F is above F*, consider the reverse trade.

Check executable prices: sell at the bid and buy at the ask. For each spot or forward midpoint, bid = midpoint × (1 − spread / 20,000) and ask = midpoint × (1 + spread / 20,000), with spread in basis points.

Borrowing USD works only if S_bid × (1 + r_HKD × T) / F_ask exceeds 1 + r_USD × T. Borrowing HKD works only if F_bid × (1 + r_USD × T) / S_ask exceeds 1 + r_HKD × T. Otherwise choose no profitable trade.
