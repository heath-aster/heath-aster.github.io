Step 1 of 3: Check Parity & Direction

Round 1 uses simplified classroom quotes and a full year (360/360): HKD interest is 10% and USD interest is 5%. Try the cash flows with US$100 first, then scale up to US$1,000,000.

Show hint: compare covered returns

Use T = days / 360 and annual rates as decimals. With HKD per USD quotes, the midpoint benchmark is F* = S × (1 + r_HKD × T) / (1 + r_USD × T), where S is the spot midpoint.

If F is below F*, consider borrowing USD, selling USD spot, investing HKD, and buying USD forward to repay. If F is above F*, consider the reverse trade.

Check executable prices: sell at the bid and buy at the ask. Read all four prices directly from the two order books above; there is no extra spread to apply.

Borrowing USD works only if S_bid × (1 + r_HKD × T) / F_ask exceeds 1 + r_USD × T. Borrowing HKD works only if F_bid × (1 + r_USD × T) / S_ask exceeds 1 + r_HKD × T. Otherwise choose no profitable trade.
