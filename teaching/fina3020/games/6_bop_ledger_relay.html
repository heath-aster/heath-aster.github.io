Stage 1: Open-Economy Balance Sheet Ledger

Your team is maintaining the national Balance of Payments (BPM6) ledger for a small open economy. Every single transaction requires two offsetting entries: a real/economic leg and a financing leg.

Today's 4 Real & Financial Transactions:

  1. Tx 1: Machinery Export: A domestic manufacturer exports $100M of heavy equipment. The foreign buyer pays via a bank transfer into the firm's foreign bank account.
  2. Tx 2: Financed Consumer Import: A domestic retailer imports $60M of electronics, financed by a 1-year trade credit loan from an overseas bank.
  3. Tx 3: Sovereign Bond Purchase: A domestic pension fund buys $30M of US Treasury bonds, transferring funds from its existing foreign bank deposit.
  4. Tx 4: Foreign Dividend Inflow: A domestic corporate receives $10M in dividend income from its overseas affiliate, deposited into a foreign bank account.
