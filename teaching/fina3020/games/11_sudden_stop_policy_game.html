Stage 4: Sectoral Balance Sheet Stress Debrief

Every policy choice transfers losses across balance sheets. The radar chart below breaks down how your policy mix distributed macro distress.

Policy Trade-Off Summary

  • Reserve Exhaustion vs Exchange Rate Defense: Selling reserves prevents immediate currency devaluation, but depleting reserves below critical imports triggers catastrophic unpegging.
  • Rate Hike vs Bank Solvency: Aggressive interest rate hikes attract capital but increase domestic debt service, pushing corporate borrowers into default and straining bank capital.
  • Capital Controls vs Market Access: Outflow controls preserve liquidity short-term but raise country risk premia and restrict corporate trade financing.
