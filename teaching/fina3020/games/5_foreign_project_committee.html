Exit Reflection Prompt:

Formula Reference

Colors: cash flow CFt (EUR) · FX rate · discount rate r · withholding tax w · local interest i. Rates are USD per 1 EUR. A lower spot rate means the USD has appreciated.

1. Parent NPV (Stage 2)

PVt = CFt × FXt(1 + r)t
NPV = −I × S0 + PV1 + PV2 + PV3
FXt = E[St] if unhedged, or forward rate Ft if hedged. Year 0 outlay = I × S0.

2. Hedge value

NPVhedged − NPVunhedged = Σ CFt × (Ft − E[St])(1 + r)t
The hedge adds value when the forward rate is above the expected spot rate, and costs value when it is below.

3. Policy shock (Stage 3)

EUR received in Year 3 = CF2 × (1 − w) × (1 + i)   [Case A]
EUR received in Year 3 = CF2 × (1 + i)   [Case B]
PVnew = EUR received × FX3(1 + r)3
NPVshock = NPV − PV2 + PVnew
If hedged, re-hedge the delayed euros at the Year 3 forward rate F3.

4. Expected NPV and breakeven (Stage 4)

E[NPV] = (1 − p) × NPV + p × NPVshock
p* = NPVNPV − NPVtotal loss ,   NPVtotal loss = −I × S0
p* is the highest probability of total loss at which the expected NPV stays at zero or above.
