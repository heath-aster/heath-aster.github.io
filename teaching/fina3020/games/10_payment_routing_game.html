Payment timing and working capital · about 25–30 minutes

Pay a supplier abroad.
Cover the cash gap.

Your firm must pay its supplier before its customer pays. Plan two weeks of trade: one with partners in developed markets and one with partners in emerging markets. Select a payment route and a credit line for each, then follow the cash ledger through shocks.

Each practice question has two hints and a free solution. Each decision has a hint. After practice, the page does the arithmetic. Your plan and your use of help do not affect participation credit. To complete: finish the practice, select a plan in Steps 4 and 5, play the week in Step 6, answer two questions in Step 7 and submit your reflection in Step 8.

Classroom assumptions, not market estimates. All amounts are in USD.
