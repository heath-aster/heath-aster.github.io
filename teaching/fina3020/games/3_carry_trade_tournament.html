Step-by-Step Carry Trade Math Guide

1. Total Assets Funded (V):

Portfolio Size (V) = Equity (E) × Leverage (L) = $100,000 × L

2. Borrowed JPY Debt (D):

Debt USD = V − E = $100,000 × (L − 1)
Debt JPY = Debt USD × 150.00 JPY/USD

3. Weighted Target Yield (r_target):

r_target = (w_USD × 5.00%) + (w_BRL × 12.00%) + (w_MXN × 11.00%)

4. Expected Return on Equity (ROE):

ROE = (Annual Interest Income − Annual JPY Debt Cost) ÷ Equity
ROE Formula = L × r_target − (L − 1) × 0.50%

Pedagogical Takeaway: Why Carry Profits Are Not Arbitrage

The carry trade yields positive average excess returns during quiet periods because high-yield currencies carry crash risk and negative skewness. When market panics occur, funding currencies (JPY) rapidly appreciate while target currencies collapse, wiping out years of accumulated interest carry.
