Core Misconception Debrief

Common Misconception: Students assume any non-zero cross-currency basis represents a frictionless, infinite arbitrage profit.

Market Reality: Executing a CIP arbitrage or taking the other side of FX swaps expands both sides of dealer balance sheets. When non-risk-weighted leverage ratios ($x \le \bar{X}$) bind, dealer balance-sheet space becomes scarce and costly. Arbitrageurs cannot eliminate a persistent basis without consuming scarce leverage capacity!
